No myDAQ?

mybench opens a simulated one, with a test circuit wired to it. Every instrument works.

Firmware

The myDAQ needs a current NI firmware. One that was used once with a recent NI ELVISmx or NI-DAQmx has it. mybench never writes to the device.

Beta

Tested with a real myDAQ on macOS and Linux; Windows on the simulator so far. A few students are testing it. Report what breaks.
